# Limitations and Responsible Interpretation

1. **2026 financials are budget/provisional.** They are not directly equivalent to final audited historical World Cup actuals.
2. **Projected surplus is analytical.** Revenue/budget less World Cup cost is not presented as certified accounting profit.
3. **Audience metrics differ by definition.** Reach, engagement, video views, impressions and final-match audience are not interchangeable.
4. **Missing historical digital data remains missing.** The project does not replace unavailable observations with zero.
5. **Venue capacity is event-specific.** Tournament-era capacity was reviewed because stadium configurations can change by edition.
6. **Fan experience is only partially measurable.** Attendance and engagement cannot by themselves prove affordability, easy travel, visa accessibility or subjective satisfaction.
7. **Expansion affects totals.** Record totals are interpreted alongside average attendance, utilisation and per-match sporting metrics.
